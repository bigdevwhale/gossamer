## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-01

### Added
- Catching thoughts in two seconds: widget (write or say), quick-settings tile, icon shortcuts, «Share» and selected text from any app, voice right in the sheet through the phone's speech service.
- Four kinds of thought (idea, question, noticed, words), each drawn as its own thing caught in the web.
- The web drawn in code: threads pulled by dragging, hubs pulled in to the middle with their words, the spider running to what was just caught.
- «Caught before»: repeats are tied to the old thought with a thicker thread.
- Night catch, morning dew on old thoughts, the lure for an open question, the wind and the Fluff.
- Cocoons of five to nine thoughts that ripen for three days and hatch into butterflies with one sentence.
- Indian summer: the year's twelve thoughts in September.
- Window light by the hour and the season; Markdown export, JSON backup and restore; English and Russian.
